## Features

- Visual sales pipeline with drag-and-drop deal stages and customizable funnels
- Unified contact and company records with interaction history and activity logging
- Task and reminder scheduling with calendar view and deadline notifications
- Built-in email integration for sending templates and tracking client conversations
- Reporting dashboard with conversion metrics, revenue forecasts, and exportable reports
